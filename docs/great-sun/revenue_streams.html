Services Revenue

The credex-core API offers the central functions available to all members on the payment terms above. All other features handled by client applications can be subject to other fees. These clients apps can be built by anyone, and offer members alternative ways to interact with other members and with their underlying data in the credex ecosystem.

Basic account management functions are simple for client apps to handle, and free options will enable members to perform all basic account functions without additional charges. Great Sun Group's VimbisoPay app is the first of such clients.

There will be multiple ways for client apps and services to add value, and Great Sun Group will take advantage of revenue-generating opportunites across the areas where we can quickly add the most value and demonstrate business models for:

  • VimbisoPay: cashin/cashout, payment processing functions like batch processing, payroll deductions, client management.
  • Vimbiso: crytographically secure and convenient data management for individuals and businesses.
  • Great Sun Financial: investment products.
  • Great Sun Marketing Suite: tools to connect business owners and marketing teams to potential customers in the credex ecosystem.

This second set of revenue streams is not yet presented in detail, as our first priority is to establish stable membership revenue. Our business model and our path to profitability are built on that foundation. These services are blue horizons for future growth in a space in which we are intimately familiar and even our competitors will rely on our expertise.
