Trust in Secured Credex

Think of the credex ecosystem as a network of community savings clubs, but with modern technology linking them together, verifying their accounting data, and making it safer and more trustworthy than ever. Here's how we protect your money.

The Bank Statement Promise

  • Every day, every credex trust account publishes its bank statements showing exactly how much money is being held
  • For every secured credex dollar in your account, there's a real dollar in an audited trust account
  • You and anyone else can check the audit statements - it's like having the whole community watching over all the money

Double-Entry Protection

  • It's like having two trusted elders keeping separate record books
  • Every time money moves, both books are required to match perfectly
  • If even one cent is off, we know immediately and can fix it, and the community can easily enforce accountability based on auditable facts

Instant Rollback Safety

  • If anything goes wrong, we can "rewind the tape" to the last known good point
  • It's like having a backup copy of every transaction
  • Your money is never at risk because we can always just look back and correct discrepancies and improve the code for next time

Community Verification

  • Just like how market traders know who to trust by reputation
  • Our system is open for anyone to verify the total amounts
  • While your personal balance stays private, the total money in the system is public
  • It's like having the whole market watching over their collective savings

Real Asset Backing

  • Every secured credex dollar is backed by a real dollar in a trust account
  • Every secured credex ounce of gold is backed by a real ounce of gold in a real vault (coming soon)
  • We don't play games with your money
  • You can cash out anytime because the money is always there
  • Trust accounts can be local or international for resilience and protection.
  • On launch, Nova Scotian company Great Sun Financial Inc.manages the only audited account, with a trust account managed by VimbisoPay Zimbabwe opening soon
  • Your money is always just a transfer away via Mukuru or other provider.
  • You can diversify your assets across trust accounts in any way you want, and even open your own and become a hub for cash in / cash out of the credex ecosystem (coming soon)
  • Even without an audited bank account, you can become a street hub for cash in / cash out. If you have a secured balance you can issue it to others in exchange for cash, or anything else.

Solving the Small Change Crisis

  • When you keep your change as secured credex, it's always safe and available
  • No more need to accept candies and pens that you don't need
  • Your secured credex change is as good as real dollars, but easier to use
  • You can send exact amounts, no matter how small
  • Funded by monthly memberships starting at $1, credex has zero transaction fees, ever

Remember: This isn't just another mobile money system. This is your money, protected by technology but working like the community savings clubs you already trust. The only difference is that now you and everyone can see proof every day that your money is safe, and you never have to worry about whether the change will be there when you need it.
