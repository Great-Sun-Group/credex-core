### Market Battle Scenes - Visual Direction Guide

#### Layer Mechanics
We're depicting three simultaneous layers of reality that interact but remain distinct:

1. **Human Layer (Surface Reality)**
- Crisp, naturally lit, documentary-style footage
- Focus on authentic market activities
- Subtle environmental effects from battles below
- Humans react to economic impacts without seeing causes
- Camera moves smoothly through crowds
- Ghost-like force labels barely visible
- Environmental reflections of power signatures

2. **Battle Layer (Invisible Reality)**
- Highly stylized, dynamic action sequences
- Energy effects and power signatures
- Dramatic lighting from combat effects
- Fast-paced camera movements
- Occupies same space as human layer but offset
- Full visibility of force labels and effects
- Dynamic label interactions with combat

3. **Network Layer (Underground Reality)**
- Abstract digital/mystical environment
- Flowing data streams and value exchanges
- Safe zones glow with golden light (#FBB016)
- Corrupted zones pulse with darkness
- Camera moves freely through impossible space
- Data-integrated force labels
- System architecture labels

#### Transition Techniques

**Market-to-Battle Transitions**
- Camera passes through market stall walls
- Reality peels back like pages
- Market sounds fade to battle acoustics
- Colors shift from natural to power palettes
- Human figures become transparent

**Battle-to-Network Transitions**
- Ground dissolves into digital landscape
- Market structures transform into data architecture
- Battle effects leave trails in network space
- Power signatures echo through layers
- Reality bends and folds
