Credex & Great Sun

Great Sun Financial Inc., registered in Nova Scotia, Canada, currently owns and manages the credex ecosystem developed by founder Ryan Watson with support of investors and team members. Currently operating as a private corporation to drive initial development and growth, this is an arrangement with a committed endpoint: on December 21, 2040, ownership will transition to the democratically governed Credex Foundation.

Until that transition, Great Sun will develop and maintain the ecosystem, managing a tiered fee structure to cover costs and generate returns for investors, while fostering an environment that encourages third-party innovation. After the transition, Great Sun will continue to operate as one of many service providers in the ecosystem, competing on equal terms with other fintech companies. This unique approach balances the need for rapid innovation and growth with the long-term goal of democratic governance of the economic system.

The Dialectic

Initial technical development and operations of the credex ecosystem are based in a private corporate structure. This structure has provided the tools to incentivize financial support (investment) for development and prototyping, and it also provides proven tools to manage rapid ecosystem growth from technical, marketing, and strategic perspectives.

However, core to the impulse that has led to the development of the credex ecosystem is the belief that the underlying structure of an economy's medium of exchange should not be owned and managed for profit. The underlying architecture of the system should support every member equally in their efforts to generate profit. Having the architecture of an economic system itself managed for the profit of specific members of the system gradually shifts the system itself for the benefit of those members. This is an influence that over time has become a major contributor to the devastated current state of the global economy.

Financialization is good for an economy, and over-financialization is bad for an economy. If the financiers manage the financial architecture that we all depend on, then incentives become misaligned, and the economy will move towards over-financialization for the benefit of the few. When that happend eventually everyone suffers the consequences.

  • A business is appropriately managed for the benefit of its owners (shareholders) by fulfilling the needs of its customers. For-profit corporate structures have proven able to spark and manage the kind of exponential growth that the credex ecosystem is aiming for in the coming years. We are providers of a networked, cloud-based, software as a service, and although our purpose is as unique as our software, there are multitudes of existing tools, experiences, and corporate models that we can draw on.
  • An economy is appropriately managed for the benefit of its participants (citizens), who each deserve equal access to the financial architecture, and equal treatment within it. Democratic decision-making processes focused on the decisions that affect all are the only way to ensure that the core architecture remains tuned to the needs of all.

Our Strategy

Our strategy balances within this dialectic by conceptually establishing the position of the democratic Credex Foundation from the start, and gradually forming the democratic processes that will enable governance to be turned over to the Foundation in the years ahead.

We are not starting with fully actualized democratic governance processes becuase we are not attempting a thought experiment. We are attempting to launch a real system that can actually be used by a handful of people and then scale massively. Utility is what matters first. Time spent considering the ins and outs of how we might democratically govern the system in some far off and different state at the scale we hope to achieve adds nothing to the process of actually getting us to that destination.

We prototype, build, grow, assess, pivot, and experiment until we have something that has achieved the scale where our governance must be upgraded, and we upgrade as needed.

As long as investors, members, and the public are clear on the path we are taking towards our shared goals, the dialectic is manageable, and in fact very much breaks in our favor.

Corporate Commitment from Great Sun

On December 21, 2040, Great Sun will turn over legal ownership and full governance control of the credex ecosystem to a functioning Credex Foundation that structurally plants democratic oversight and decision making to protect the rights and enforce the obligations of all equally.

Until the transition, Great Sun will manage a fee structure of free and paid tiers, and revenue from paid tiers will cover development and deployment of the base architecture and will also generate profit for the company's shareholders.

At the transition, the Credex Foundation will take over responsibility for the expenses of the base architecture, and for covering those costs in a democratically decided way. There may still be costs to individuals associated with accessing the ecosystem, but these costs will be fully removed from the market economy and decided democratically. Once the transition is completed, Great Sun Group will be identical to all other fintechs within the ecosystem, with no special privileges or revenues.

We have done everything in our power to design our membership fee structure in a way that provides access to these tools at a low and accessible cost to anyone. We aim to add value far above our membership fees, so that the cost can quickly be recouped by the value that our members receive. We are a company that competes in the financial services market. We provide broad access to our product as a growth-focused business strategy that is fully aligned with our aspirations for financial inclusion. We aspire to do all our business in a manner where everyone wins.

As a fintech corporation, we are fully dedicated to the financial empowerment and economic betterment of our customers and members. We remain attentive to the community needs of our members as well as their individual needs as our customers, and we direct our charitable giving and our hiring practices towards the communities where our members live.

Democratic Stability & Private Innovation

The fintech innovation of the credex ecosystem occurred in a private corporation, and fintech innovation within the credex ecosystem is also expected to occur in private corporations. The underlying democratically governed architecture is meant for stability, whereas innovation requires flexibility. Our approach to handle this dialectic remains aligned with the above. By enabling private and for-profit innovation within the democratically governed architecture of the credex ecosystem, these innovators can connect to stable rails while remaining fully in control of their own software, services, environments, and business models.

This approach is crystallized in our license, which allows the development of private client apps that connect to the main credex-core API. Users of these client apps will be required to be members of the credex ecosystem, and the apps are allowed to charge their users for the functionality they provide over and above what is offered by the ecosystem API.

In addition to our position managing the core infrastructure until the transition, Great Sun Group will be at the forefront of fintech innovation within the credex ecosystem, and will build revenue streams by delivering the gold standard of financial services to credex members.

But while Great Sun will be the only company that receives membership revenue from ecosystem membership fees until transition, when it comes to all other services, Great Sun will soon be in competition with other service providers.

This strategy feeds the base revenue for Great Sun, and by opening third party development we open the potential for mutually beneficial partnerships across the financial and technology sectors. Being poised to onboard the customer bases of other financial institutions and fintech companies by offering them services they can't get anywhere else is part of our positioning for exponential growth.
