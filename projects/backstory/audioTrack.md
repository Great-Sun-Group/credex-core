- Power signatures affect voice tones
- Battle dialogue sharp and urgent
- Victory speeches inspiring and warm

Music Structure:
- Opens with traditional financial sounds
- Builds tension through battles
- Incorporates African rhythms in Zimbabwe
- Triumphant fusion score for resolution
