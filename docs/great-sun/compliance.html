Regulatory Compliance

Accounting Framework

The credex ecosystem implements a GAAP-compliant double-entry accounting system where each credex transaction creates matching accounts payable and accounts receivable entries for the counterparties involved. This ensures:

  • Complete audit trails for all transactions
  • Accurate tracking of income and expenses
  • Proper balance sheet representation
  • Clear documentation of value exchanges

Example Accounting Entries

When a credex is issued and accepted, the following entries are automatically generated:

Account Debit Credit
Issuer's Accounts Payable Transaction Amount
Issuer's Expense Account Transaction Amount
Acceptor's Accounts Receivable Transaction Amount
Acceptor's Revenue Account Transaction Amount

When a credex is cleared in a credloop, adjusting entries are made:

Account Debit Credit
Accounts Payable Cleared Amount
Accounts Receivable Cleared Amount

Tax Compliance

The credex ecosystem is designed to ensure full tax compliance across all jurisdictions. Key aspects include:

Value Recognition

  • All value exchanges are recorded at fair market value
  • Income is recognized when value is provided (accrual basis)
  • Expenses are recognized when value is received
  • Exchange rates are updated daily through the DCO process

Tax Reporting

  • Complete transaction histories available for tax reporting
  • Multi-currency support with automatic rate conversions
  • Clear distinction between business and personal transactions
  • Detailed records of all value exchanges for tax assessment

Tax Authority Access

Any entity with authority to audit financial records for tax purposes has equivalent authority to audit credex transactions. The system provides:

  • Comprehensive audit trails
  • Transaction verification mechanisms
  • Historical exchange rate data
  • Clear documentation of value flows

Secured Credex Compliance

Asset Backing

Secured credexes are backed by real assets held by authorized and audited institutions:

  • Daily auditing of trust accounts
  • Verification of 1:1 backing of all secured credexes
  • Segregation of secured assets by denomination
  • Regular compliance checks of securing institutions

Issuance Controls

The system implements strict controls on secured credex issuance:

  • Only authorized institutions can issue unrestricted secured credexes
  • Other accounts limited to previously received secured balances
  • Automated verification of securing assets
  • Real-time monitoring of secured credex circulation

Clearing Mechanisms

Secured credexes maintain denomination isolation in clearing:

  • Separate credloop paths for each secured denomination
  • No mixing of secured and unsecured credexes in loops
  • Automated clearing through the MTQ process
  • Full audit trails of all clearing operations
