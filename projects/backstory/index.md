### Output
We are creating the first presentation of our origin story. Our output will be a 30-40 second video that introduces our storyverse, our superheroes and dark forces, and ending with two vendors in a Harare street market picking up the superpower tool created by our superheroes and activating it through their smartphones, connecting them to each other and a now-secured and comfortable underground global network of immense power, where they are greeted by digital representations of our superhero team as guides.

This first video of our origin story will be a little like a movie preview, or maybe more like a flashback. It will include high action and crazy superpower moves and in iconic financial locations. There will be a dark time when evil looks all-consuming, but our heroic team delivers at the last moment by escaping into the superpower tool they have been forging through the whole video. The tool connects them to the Zimbabwe street markets and puts some of their superpowers at the service of market vendors.

### VimbisoPay and its Archnemesis
VimbisoPay is a global semi-mythical superhero team with lifespans measured in centuries that has passed the fight on from generation to generation stretching back over the five thousand or more years that these superheroes have been battling their arch-nemeses on this plane of the physical Earth. The team is currently led by four core members that fight at home and abroad for African small business owners against outside neocolonial dominance and corrupt local politics, which in our storyverse are currently the most horrific expressions of the implacable forces of the evil and life-destroying Deflation and Corruption.

All members of the team are always armed, though sometimes only subtly. They stand guard in a protective role, focusing the power of the entire African continent, with clear support from others who struggle with these same forces all around the world, as a symbol that holds the vast forces arrayed against the success of the African entrepreneur at bay. These forces can wipe out any entrepreneur anywhere with casual ease, and African entrepreneurs are their current favorite prey, and the minions of these evil creatures are loosed to feast on the wealth of destroyed African businesses and lives. The VimbisoPay team creates spaces that are safe from the intense pressures and oppressive conditions that these small businesses have no way to handle on their own.

On a level playing field of fair African and global business-to-business competition, the VimbisoPay team never takes sides. But when the economic playing field tilts towards the powerful at the expense of the vulnerable as Deflation and Corruption work their insidious magic, VimbisoPay burns through corruption and parasitic middlemen with righteous fury and an effectiveness that is awe-inspiring to us but often almost casual to our superheroes, who expose the weakness of the dark forces for all to see. And yet the dark forces have built strength for so long that all seems lost.

### Premise
In our storyverse, the human system of value exchange has a literal existence as a magical underground space that can potentially connect any point on Earth directly to any other point through a secure, instant, mysterious transportation and communication network. This network looks and behaves exactly like a secure data exchange network built on modern technologies of graph databases, blockchain, mobile devices, and cloud-based apps and APIs, but in our storyverse there are two important differences:
1. This network has existed forever, but humans have only ever been able to use it to the extent that our superheroes have been able to protect it by carving safe space from the forces that stalk this underground space.
2. Our superheroes can physically travel through the network, in journeys through harrowing landscapes and efforts to carve safe spaces for human value exchanges in this magical space, and transport to any location on physical earth.

### The Dark Forces and the Present Moment
Deflation and Corruption, the dark forces of our storyverse, seem to originate from the raw state of the underground network, which is visually dark and terrifying, with its own theme music and cinematic vibe. These forces have to be beaten back and the territory claimed by our heroes in order to be safe for human value exchange. Claimed territory looks different over the centuries and cultures, representative of the business cultures and locations and networks of its time and place, and is always in danger of being lost to the dark forces. Our superheroes must always be vigilant and do not always win. There are losses that cause centuries of human misery. We have not had such a period in centuries, but one is upon us. Deflation and Corruption stalk our streets.

Our superheroes have helped the modern money system connect massive parts of the global economy, for the significant benefit of all (and the massively outsized benefit of the few), but Deflation and Corruption have gnawed the foundation in recent decades to the point where everything is set to crumble and shatter, with Deflation and Corruption taking back vast swaths of the global economy and devouring the safe spaces, turning them once again to terror and darkness that is poisonous to human value exchange.

Our heroes have seen this coming for decades, and after failing to help humans make the choices that might have averted this disaster using the tools and powers of the existing global financial infrastructure, in recent decades they have put their efforts into a secret weapon that will give the brave a chance to persevere and succeed in the most adverse of conditions. The story of the building of this weapon and the conquering of the safe spaces it protects is the origin story of our team of VimbisoPay heroes.

Within the safe space their powers have just now successfully forged, the VimbisoPay team offers storyverse superpowers to African entrepreneurs. Each of these iconic storyverse superpowers or artifacts is related to a non-storyverse service provided by the credex ecosystem. These offered storyverse superpowers are not as great as those of our heroes, but they do multiply the entrepreneurs' own storyverse strengths so they can win their own battles and build their own successes on a level playing field.

The success and the struggles of real entrepreneurs will be reflected in the stories of the character subplots of our storyverse. The iconic superpowers offered to entrepreneurs during storylines in the storyverse will accurately share an aspect of what the credex ecosystem is capable of providing as a service in the non-storyverse world.
