Core Architecture

The processes of creating an account, logging in, arranging queries for the API endpoints, and processing query results for human-friendly presentation and interaction is managed by a client apps client apps such as the VimbisoPay mobile app and the vimbiso-chatserver provided by VimbisoPay Africa.

Services

The accounting and data management services provided by the credex ecosystem establish a network protocol for financial sovereignty:

  • Secured credex services enable our sovereign members to base their accounting entries on the trustworthy foundation of real assets such as currency or gold.
  • Unsecured credex services provide liquidity to meet all needs and fuel strong credit-based economic growth when conditions warrant, as determined by the distributed risk tolerance and risk/reward calculations of economic participants.
  • Credcoin services mathematically lock all values in the credex ecosystem to the market values of real natural resource flows, keeping the economy oriented on the most meaningful reference point possible without limiting systemic liquidity, personal choice, or financial innovation.

Credex Principle

If I owe you,
and you owe them,
and they owe me...
we're square.

The credex ecosystem actualizes the Credex Principle in the Minute Transaction Queue (MTQ), which tracks payable and receivable invoices for members' accounts, finds loops of value creation, and cancels payable and receivable invoices against each other in credloops in which every debt is reduced by an equal value. This is a GAAP-compliant accounting procedure that anyone and any business can benefit from, anywhere in the world.

In accounting terms, an outstanding credex is a contingent asset for one party and a contingent liability for the other. These asset/liability pairs are automatically strung together into credloops wherever possible across the credex ecosystem. When a credloop is found and cleared, every account in the loop will have an accounts payable invoice cleared against an equivalent accounts receivable invoice.

This behaviour enables members to replace the use of third party money or other debt instruments that are issued by an outside sovereign with accounting entries on a shared ledger that are backed only by the sovereign credit, assets, and value-producing capacities of the transacting members.

Unsecured Credex Services

The credex ecosystem provides the non-sovereign and non-monetary services of:

  • Verifying the identity of the sovereign human counterparties and their corporations.
  • Managing the technical verification and standardization of the credex invoice and contract between the counterparties by providing templates that include conditions for fulfillment, default, and other states, and actions executed on each condition or state change.
  • Executing these credex smart contract invoices according to the terms agreed by the counterparties, including triggering the agreed follow on actions dependent on the invoice state and outcome.

These invoice clearing services of the MTQ empower a first layer of distributed financial sovereignty by enabling us to issue our own customizable smart contracts. In so doing, we replace our reliance on the currencies of outside sovereigns and give ourselves options that insulate us from the financial dislocations of inflation and deflation in those currencies.

Credcoin Principle

I am entitled to receive one equal share
of the value our economy harvests from nature.

I am responsible to pay for the share
of natural wealth that I consume.

The credex ecosystem actualizes the Credcoin Principle for participating members in the Daily Credcoin Offering (DCO), in a process which anchors relative exchange rates across the ecosystem. Every 24h, the DCO makes this equation true: one credcoin today equals the number of members participating in the DCO today divided by the value of natural wealth entering the organic economy through the DCO today.

The DCO is a market where those who consume more natural resources purchase the right to do so fairly from those who consume less:

  1. DCO participants pay in at rates relative to the natural resources they consume.
  2. One credcoin (1.000 CXX) is issued daily per DCO participant, each worth one equal share of the total value received.
  3. This process establishes the value of 1.000 CXX for the day relative to all other economic values.

The credex ecosystem uses the flows of value that are created by members participating in the DCO as the anchor point for exchange rates across the ecosystem. These flows, which can be as small as a single member, provide a mathematical peg that anchors financial reality in the credex ecosystem to the reality of underlying natural resource flows.

The multi-denominational mathematical mechanisms of the DCO manage the dynamic balance between:

  • Local truth: preserving the value of all contracts in their specified denomination.
  • Global truth: preserving the value of all contracts relative to every other value.
  • Past truth: preserving the value of past contracts relative to today's real resource flow values.
  • Future truth: preserving the value of all contracts independent of changing resource flow values.

The credex ecosystem stores all values in credcoin (CXX). In order to natively enable multi-denominational accounting, a CXXmultiplier value is stored alongside every CXX value. Both of these values are updated in every DCO so that all accounting and reporting remains universally true both to the face value in the denomination specified, and to all other values stored in the ecosystem. A contract expresses it's CXX value when it needs a reference vs any other value in the ecosystem. A contract expresses it's face value when it needs a reference vs the original intentions of the counterparties. On-the-fly exchange rate calculations performed on CXX values enable any amount to be viewed or totaled in the current or past value of any denomination at any time.

Credcoin Services

The credex ecosystem provides the non-sovereign and non-monetary services of:

  • Processing and distributing the daily flows of value that actualize the entitlements and responsibilities of the Credex Principle for participating sovereign members.
  • Setting the relative values of all contracts in all denominations daily by linking global market data to CXX through the real flows of value occuring in the DCO.
  • Establishing CXX as the first formula-based denomination of the credex ecosystem, anchored on the rights and responsibilities of universal human sovereignty.
  • Providing a container for other formulaic denominations and financial innovations designed by for-profit and non-profit corporations for their own purposes and reliably linked to real economic values through the process of the DCO.

These credcoin services empower a second layer of distributed financial sovereignty by connecting every member to the underlying resource reality of their financial decisions, and relating every other measure of value across the economy to that underlying reality. In this way we replace our reliance on measures of value that are created and controlled by obsolete monetary paradigms with a measure of value that is structurally locked to the daily flows of real natural resources that sustain us, while still empowering full flexibility for financial innovation and experimentation in the private sector.

Secured Credexes

The credex ecosystem clears debts against each other. A secured credex balance is no different, but can only be created when a member purchases a secured credex from an Trust account. These secured credexes are accounting entrees just like unsecured credexes. Every credex is a debt contract, fulfilled when credlooped. For secured credexes, cash out always triggers a credloop and debt clearing, thus fulfilling their promise of financial security and 100% backing.

The credex ecosystem does not process currency, hold funds, or come into contact with money in any way. Secured credexes are not produced by the credex ecosystem, they are "produced" when real assets get audit-bound into the ecosystem and they are "destroyed" in equal measure when those assets leave those audited accounts into the direct possession of members. Credex is not money, credex is peer-to-peer accounting.

Secured Credex Services

The credex ecosystem provides the non-sovereign and non-monetary services of:

  • Auditing of secured credex Trust accounts so that a secured credex is never fractionalized or abstracted.
  • Only allowing unaudited accounts to issue secured credex in denominations and values that they have previously received into their account balances.
  • Isolating secured credexes of each denomination into their own search substructure so that they only close in loops with other secured credexes of the same denomination, ensuring that the link from real assets to secured balances remains intact and auditable.
